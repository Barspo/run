# Greek wedding dance

A 10 second cartoon of three friends dancing sirtaki at a Greek island wedding.
Everything in it is generated by code — no stock footage, no image assets.

    pip install pillow numpy imageio-ffmpeg
    python3 make_video.py            # -> greek_wedding_dance.mp4

Output: 1280x720, 25 fps, H.264 + AAC, ~3 MB.

## What the script does

* **Scenery** — night sky and stars, a moon over the sea, a Cycladic village on
  the horizon, terrace wall, bougainvillea, an olive tree, a drinks table, and a
  tiled dance floor in perspective. Drawn once and reused for every frame.
* **String lights** — three sagging strands that sway, with bulbs that twinkle
  independently and cast additive glow onto the frame.
* **Dancers** — three rigged figures in a shoulder-hold line. Each pose is
  solved per frame: hips, torso, head, and two-bone IK for the knees and elbows.
  Outfits are matched to the photos this was made from (cream camp-collar shirt
  and a whisky glass, black maxi dress with a slit, beige button-down and black
  sneakers).
* **The dance** — the classic four-beat sirtaki figure: sway, cross, kick right,
  kick left, with each dancer offset slightly so the movement ripples down the
  line. Like a real sirtaki the tempo accelerates, 96 → 186 BPM.
* **Soundtrack** — synthesized from scratch with numpy: a bouzouki-ish plucked
  riff in D hijaz over a bass line and hand claps, on the same accelerating beat
  grid the dancers use, plus a cheer on the "opa".
* **Finish** — confetti burst, a flash of light and ΩΠΑ! at 7.5 s, over a slow
  push-in that runs the whole clip.

## Tweaking

Timing and framing live in the constants at the top (`DUR`, `FPS`, `BPM0`,
`BPM1`, `OPA_T`). The three characters are the `CAST` list — colours, hair,
outfit and props are all data there. To iterate on a single moment without
rendering the whole thing:

    python3 make_video.py --preview 40 130 200     # writes preview_NNN.png
