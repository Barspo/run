Research date: 17 November 2026. Currency: USD converted to ILS at 3.7 ILS/USD (as instructed). Prices quoted "+VAT" mean before Israeli VAT.

## Q1. How many businesses exist in Israel, by size and legal form, and how many advertise on Meta?

### Takeaway
Israel had about 685,000 active businesses in 2024. About 57% are one-person businesses with no employees, and about 294,000 SMEs have 1–99 employees. No public source gives the number of Israeli businesses advertising on Facebook or Instagram, so that figure has to be estimated (see Q7).

### Cited Findings
- **HARD DATA (2024):** Israel had about 685,000 businesses in 2024. 99.5% of them were SMEs. — [OECD, Financing SMEs and Entrepreneurs 2026: Israel profile](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/financing-smes-and-entrepreneurs-2026_075d8058-en/full-report/israel_e39ce641.html)
- **HARD DATA (2024), size split (same OECD profile):**
  - Independent workers with no employees: 56.6% of businesses.
  - Micro (1–4 employees): 30.4% of businesses, 11.2% of employment.
  - Small (5–19): 9.9% of businesses, 17.6% of employment.
  - Medium (20–99): 2.6% of businesses, 20.1% of employment.
  — [OECD 2026](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/financing-smes-and-entrepreneurs-2026_075d8058-en/full-report/israel_e39ce641.html)
- **HARD DATA (2024), turnover of firms:** About 61,000 businesses/companies were established in 2024 and about 48,000 closed. — [OECD 2026](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/financing-smes-and-entrepreneurs-2026_075d8058-en/full-report/israel_e39ce641.html)
- **HARD DATA (2023–2024), Israel Small and Medium Business Agency (SBA, Ministry of Economy):**
  - More than 700,000 SMEs (up to 100 employees), which is 99.6% of all businesses.
  - They employ about 60% of business-sector employees and produce about 50% of business-sector output.
  — [SBA about page](https://www.sba.org.il/hb/PolicyAndInformation/Pages/about.aspx); [SBA 2024 activity summary](https://www.sba.org.il/hb/Pages/prSum24.aspx)
- **HARD DATA (2023):** The SBA periodic report table gives a total of 701,397 businesses, broken down by employee bands. The search-extracted table was garbled, so the band counts were not captured. — [SBA periodic report 2023 (PDF)](https://www.sba.org.il/hb/PolicyAndInformation/Researches/Documents/SBAreport2023.pdf)
- **HARD DATA (end-2023), exempt dealers (עוסק פטור):**
  - About 422,000 exempt dealers at end-2023, up 45% since 2019.
  - Licensed dealers (עוסק מורשה) grew only 13% over the same period.
  - Source: TheMarker, using Tax Authority data. These counts are registrations, not necessarily active businesses.
  — [TheMarker, 21 Mar 2024](https://www.themarker.com/career/2024-03-21/ty-article-magazine/.premium/0000018e-5fd1-d411-a3df-dfdbfd220000)
- **HARD DATA (2021):** 183,294 *active* exempt dealers in 2021, up 26.7% from 2017. This much lower "active" count conflicts with the 422,000 registered figure above. — [Knesset Research and Information Center](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/f5014c7e-c8e2-ed11-8157-005056aac6c3/2_f5014c7e-c8e2-ed11-8157-005056aac6c3_11_20067.pdf)
- The exempt-dealer turnover ceiling was ₪120,000 in 2024. Some professions (lawyers, accountants and others) must register as licensed dealers whatever their turnover. — [Ziv Shifer CPA](https://www.zscpa.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A7-%D7%A4%D7%98%D7%95%D7%A8/)
- **Not comparable:** Companydata.com counts 1,075,764 registered companies, active and inactive. Do not mix this with active-business counts. — [Companydata](https://companydata.com/israel/)
- **Digital/AI adoption (2025–2026):**
  - About 39% of Israeli businesses used AI in March 2026, up from 28% in June 2025, per CBS business-tendency data as reported by secondary sources. — [STS Iconic summary of CBS data](https://stsiconic.com/39-%D7%9E%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C-%D7%9B%D7%91%D7%A8-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%9D-ai-%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%A0/); [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/20031033)
  - SBA's 2025 survey on AI found use among 31% of self-employed, 26% of micro businesses and 18% of small businesses.
  - Among SMEs that use AI, it is mainly for writing (70%), graphics (~50%) and data analysis (~33%).
  — [SBA research: AI use among businesses](https://www.sba.org.il/hb/PolicyAndInformation/Researches/Pages/sr90.aspx)
  - An Israel Democracy Institute survey (July 2025) found 28% of businesses reporting AI use in the past half-year. — [IDI](https://www.idi.org.il/media/28643/practical-adoption-of-ai-in-business.pdf)
- **Meta advertisers in Israel:** No source found. No official or credible count of Israeli businesses advertising on Meta turned up in English or Hebrew searches. An agency blog's claim of "hundreds of thousands of businesses" using Meta platforms has no stated method. — [search result summary; no reliable source](https://talsa.co.il/meta-ads-smallbusiness/)

### Inferences
- Derived from OECD 2024 shares × 685,000 (my arithmetic):
  - No-employee businesses ≈ 388,000.
  - Micro (1–4 employees) ≈ 208,000.
  - Small (5–19) ≈ 68,000.
  - Medium (20–99) ≈ 17,800.
  - SMEs with employees (1–99) ≈ 294,000.
  - Large (100+) ≈ 3,400.
- The ~294,000 employer SMEs plus the more commercial part of the ~388,000 solo businesses are the natural universe for Marta. Many solo businesses are freelancers or side businesses, such as tech contractors, that do not advertise on Meta.
- About 48,000 closures a year against about 685,000 businesses is roughly 7% a year. That sets a floor for logo churn in any SMB SaaS, before any product-driven churn.
- SMB AI use is rising fast (28% to 39% in under a year) but is lower in small firms (18–26%). An AI interface is becoming familiar, but "AI" alone will not sell to the smallest firms. The pitch should be about outcomes (leads, sales).

### Gaps
- The CBS Business Register 2024/2025 table (active businesses by size and legal form) could not be retrieved directly. The OECD figures are the best proxy.
- No current count of *active* exempt vs licensed dealers for 2024/2025.
- No count of Meta advertisers in Israel. Meta does not publish country advertiser counts, and the Meta Ad Library was not queried.

## Q2. How large is Israel's digital advertising market, and what share goes to Meta and Google?

### Takeaway
Sources disagree widely on market size. Israeli ad-tracking data (Yifat/Bakara) puts the total ad market at about ₪4–5B, with digital at about half or more by 2023. Global estimators put 2025 digital spend at $0.9–1.6B. Google takes about 42–46% of digital and Meta about 15–25%. That puts Meta spend in Israel at roughly ₪0.6–0.9B a year (an estimate).

### Cited Findings
- **Total ad market (Yifat/Bakara, older):**
  - 2021: record ₪4.6B, up 13%. — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/advertismentandmarketing/news/article/801482)
  - 2020: about ₪4.1B, down 7%, with digital up 6%. — [ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/advertising-marketing/news/article/807225)
  - No 2024 or 2025 total was found.
- **Digital share of total:**
  - 2022: digital was about half the ad pie and TV about 40% (Bakara data). — [TheMarker, Jan 2023](https://www.themarker.com/advertising/2023-01-19/ty-article/.premium/00000185-c5eb-d3a8-a3cf-c7fb0bc60000)
  - 2023: digital about 54% (Knesset research center using Bakara data; the extracted table was partly garbled). — [Knesset RIC](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/c064d430-97e0-ee11-8162-005056aa4246/2_c064d430-97e0-ee11-8162-005056aa4246_11_20614.pdf)
- **Google and Meta shares (2023):**
  - Google held 42% of the Israeli ad pie (Bakara), with 1.1% growth in its budgets. — [TheMarker, 8 Feb 2024](https://www.themarker.com/advertising/2024-02-08/ty-article-magazine/.premium/0000018d-84b5-d424-abad-9ffd8c1f0000)
  - Of Israeli *digital* advertising, Google held 42% and Meta 25%. — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/business/tech/article-1126831)
- **2025 estimates (non-Israeli estimators):**
  - 5W Research (April 2026): digital market $1.58B in 2025, rising to $1.91B by 2028. Search ≈ $597M, social ≈ $460M, TV ≈ $260M. Google ≈ 46% and Meta ≈ 15% of digital spend. — [5W Research, AI and Israeli Marketing, Apr 2026](https://www.5wpr.com/research/ai-israeli-marketing/)
  - Statista: digital ad spend $1,581M in 2025, with search the largest segment at $659M. — [Statista Market Insights](https://www.statista.com/outlook/dmo/digital-advertising/israel)
  - Research and Markets gives a much lower 2025 base of $875.8M. — [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6217895/israel-digital-ad-spend-market-size-and-forecast)
  - The Olam (undated industry guide): total Israeli ad market ≈ ₪5–6B (~$1.5B), with TV at about 20–25% and falling. — [The Olam](https://olam.business/the-israeli-television-and-media-map)
- **Advertiser intent (2024 plans):** 75% of marketers said their largest budget would go to digital, up from 72%. This is an intention survey of the marketing community. — [shivuk.me](https://shivuk.me/news/32099)
- **Government ad spend (2023, context):** the Government Advertising Agency (Lapam) spent ₪238M in total, of which ₪113M (48%) was digital. — [ISOC-IL](https://www.isoc.org.il/sts-data/government-advertising-budget-on-social-networks)
- **Meta performance for Israeli advertisers (contested source):** Drop Site News (June 2025) reported leaked internal Meta data.
  - Israeli advertisers' cost per click rose 155% from 2023 to 2025 ($0.094 to $0.24).
  - Clicks in 2025 were 39.2% of the 2023 level.
  - Meta's revenue from Israeli advertisers fell 8% over 2023–24.
  - Not independently verified, Meta did not comment, and the coverage is politically charged. — [Drop Site News](https://www.dropsitenews.com/p/leaked-data-meta-israel-cost-per-click-marketing)
- **SMB share of digital/Meta spend:** No Israeli source found.

### Inferences
- The two Israeli digital-market bases cannot be reconciled:
  - Bakara-based: ~₪4.6B × ~54% ≈ ₪2.5B digital.
  - 5W/Statista: $1.58B ≈ ₪5.85B digital.
  - The global estimators probably define "digital" more broadly (classifieds, cross-border spend by Israeli advertisers, and so on).
- **ESTIMATE, Meta ad spend in Israel ≈ ₪0.6–0.9B a year (2023–2025):**
  - Low end: ₪2.5B × 25% ≈ ₪0.62B.
  - High end: $1.58B × 15% = $237M ≈ ₪0.88B.
- **ESTIMATE, SMB share:** assuming SMBs and small agencies account for 35–50% of Meta spend (Meta is known to be SMB-heavy, but no Israeli figure exists), SMB Meta spend ≈ ₪0.2–0.45B a year.
- If the Drop Site data is even directionally right, Israeli advertisers face rising CPCs on Meta. That raises the value of optimization help, which favors Marta's pitch, but it also pressures SMB marketing budgets.

### Gaps
- No Yifat/Bakara or IAB Israel full-year 2024 or 2025 total, digital share or Meta share was retrieved. Globes/TheMarker articles are paywalled and were not fetchable.
- No breakdown of Israeli Meta spend by advertiser size.

## Q3. How widely are social media and WhatsApp used in Israel, and how do consumers feel about receiving business messages on WhatsApp?

### Takeaway
WhatsApp reaches about 99% of Israelis, among the highest penetration anywhere, and Facebook (~90%) and Instagram (~82%) are near-universal too. TikTok reach is about 59%, but daily TikTok use fell sharply in 2026. No good Israeli consumer survey on attitudes to business WhatsApp messages was found.

### Cited Findings
- **ISOC-IL "Israel Digital Trends 2026" (survey run May 2026):**
  - Reach: WhatsApp 99%, YouTube 98%, Facebook 90%, ChatGPT 88%, Instagram 82%, TikTok 59%.
  — [ISOC-IL press release 2026](https://en.isoc.org.il/data-and-statistics/press-release-2026-social-media-ai-survey)
  - Daily TikTok use fell from 55% (2025) to 34% (2026). — [Calcalist Tech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkqhuhexfl)
- **ISOC-IL 2025 report, as reported by the Jerusalem Post:**
  - WhatsApp 99%, up about 1 point from 2024 (the article also says 98% in another passage).
  - Facebook 93% (72% daily), Instagram 81% (67% daily), TikTok 57% (55% daily).
  — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/business-and-innovation/article-871715)
- **DataReportal:**
  - Digital 2025: 6.82M social-media user identities in January 2025, 72.2% of the population. — [Digital 2025: Israel](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-israel)
  - Digital 2026: 7.01M user identities in October 2025. — [Digital 2026: Israel](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-israel)
  - Facebook ad reach ≈ 52.9% of total population at end-2025, up 250k (+5.2%) from October 2024 to October 2025.
  - Instagram ad reach ≈ 52.4% of population, up 600k (+13.6%) over the same period.
  - Ad reach is not the same as user counts. — [Digital 2026: Israel](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-israel)
- **Third-party estimates (lower confidence):**
  - Facebook+Instagram 18+ ad audience in Israel ≈ 6.47M. — [Affect Group](https://affectgroup.com/blog/israel-meta-ads-audience-breakdown-2026/)
  - Facebook 71.4% and Instagram 65.4% reach (April 2026 Ads Manager), which conflicts with DataReportal. — [businesstats](https://businesstats.com/percentage-of-selected-countries-internet-users-on-facebook/)
- **Business messaging attitudes (proxies only):**
  - An ActiveTrail-commissioned survey (about 2022) found 88% of Israelis regularly read SMS ads, 77% find SMS messages attractive, and 62% see marketing SMS from businesses as legitimate. — [ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/digital-140/news/article/920553); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/article/b12mxj15j)
  - A vendor "WhatsApp business index" (2026, bot conversation data, not an opinion survey): 46.5% of WhatsApp inquiries to small businesses arrive outside business hours, and the bot's median response time is 21 seconds. — [Achiya Automation WhatsApp index](https://achiya-automation.com/data/whatsapp-index/)

### Inferences
- WhatsApp is effectively universal, so the WhatsApp Business Platform channel needs no consumer-adoption bet in Israel. The constraint is business-side setup and cost, which Marta's "simplify Meta tools" pitch targets.
- Instagram's ad reach grew faster (+13.6%) than Facebook's (+5.2%) in 2024–25, and TikTok daily use fell. Meta remains the dominant social ad venue for Israeli SMBs in 2026.
- Nearly half of SMB WhatsApp inquiries come after hours. That supports an AI auto-reply/agent feature as a selling point (vendor data, directional only).

### Gaps
- No Israeli consumer survey found on willingness to receive *marketing* messages from businesses on WhatsApp, as opposed to SMS.
- DataReportal pages could not be opened to confirm absolute ad-reach numbers (millions) for Facebook, Instagram and Messenger.

## Q4. How many Israeli businesses use WhatsApp Business, and what does the WhatsApp Business Platform charge per message for Israel?

### Takeaway
No hard count exists of Israeli businesses on the WhatsApp Business app or API. Surveys only show strong intent: more than 60% already send marketing messages on WhatsApp, often in breach of policy. Since 1 October 2026, most third-party trackers of Meta's rate card list Israel at **$0.0353 per marketing message and $0.0053 per utility, authentication or service message**, with 1,000 free service messages per number per month. Some trackers show higher figures ($0.0406–0.0442 marketing), so the official rate card must be checked.

### Cited Findings
- **Business adoption (survey, date unclear, likely about 2022):** an IFOCUS survey of 319 self-employed people (licensed dealers or company owners) for ActiveTrail found:
  - 58% of businesses plan to advertise on WhatsApp in the coming year.
  - More than 60% already send marketing messages to customers via WhatsApp, "not always in line with WhatsApp policy".
  — [Allmarketing](https://www.allmarketing.co.il/%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%A8-58-%D7%9E%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%95-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%95%D7%90%D7%98%D7%A1%D7%90%D7%A4-%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%A8/)
- **Pricing model:** Meta moved from conversation-based to per-message pricing on 1 July 2025, adding volume tiers for utility and authentication. — [ChakraHQ](https://chakrahq.com/article/pricing-updates-for-whatsapp-business-platform-effective-july-2025-onwards/)
- **Israel rates, Meta rate card effective 1 October 2026 (per third-party trackers):**
  - Marketing **$0.0353**.
  - Utility **$0.0053**.
  - Authentication **$0.0053**.
  - Service **$0.0053**, newly charged from 1 October 2026.
  — [SleekFlow blog](https://sleekflow.io/en-us/blog/whatsapp-business-price); [Whautomate](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing); [360dialog](https://360dialog.com/blog/whatsapp-service-message-charging-october-2026/); [CampaignHQ](https://www.campaignhq.co/products/whatsapp-pricing-calculator); [Ariko](https://ariko.ai/en/guide/whatsapp-bot-cost)
- **Service message charging from 1 October 2026:**
  - Service messages are charged at the market's utility/authentication rate.
  - Each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, with no rollover and no volume tier.
  — [360dialog](https://360dialog.com/blog/whatsapp-service-message-charging-october-2026/)
  - One tracker still shows Israel service as "Free", which looks outdated. — [Zernio](https://zernio.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing)
- **The 1 July 2026 rate update reportedly left Israel unchanged.** — [Whale.co.il](https://whale.co.il/en/blog/meta-whatsapp-pricing-2026); [Srileo](https://srileo.com/tools/whatsapp-api-pricing-calculator/israel/)
- **Conflicting figures:**
  - SleekFlow's help page lists Israel at $0.0406 marketing and $0.0061 utility/authentication until 30 September 2026, and in one place $0.0406 after October. — [SleekFlow Help](https://help.sleekflow.io/whatsapp/pricing)
  - A reseller doc lists $0.0424 marketing and $0.0064 utility/authentication/service. — [Sent.dm](https://docs.sent.dm/docs/guides/whatsapp-pricing)
  - Wappbiz (an older page) lists $0.0442 marketing. — [Wappbiz](https://www.wappbiz.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-latest-pricing-update/)
  - Salesforce's 1 August 2025 rate card lists Israel at 43.59 (marketing) and 6.55 (utility/authentication) in its own units. This may include Salesforce's markup. — [Salesforce rate card Aug 2025](https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/assets/pdf/WhatsApp_Business-Messaging-Rate-Card-Aug-1-2025.pdf)
  - The euro rate card lists Israel marketing at €0.0292 from 1 October 2026. — [Tobachat](https://tobachat.nl/en/whatsapp-api-pricing-by-country/)
- **Official source to verify against:** [Meta – Pricing on the WhatsApp Business Platform](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing). It could not be fetched from this sandbox.

### Inferences
- At 3.7 ILS/USD, $0.0353 ≈ ₪0.13 per marketing message and $0.0053 ≈ ₪0.02 per utility or service message. Using the higher conflicting figures, the ranges are ₪0.13–0.16 for marketing and ₪0.02–0.024 for utility.
- Example: an SMB sending 2,000 marketing messages a month pays about $71 ≈ ₪260 to Meta, before any platform fee. This is comparable to or larger than a likely Marta subscription. Price transparency and budget caps on WhatsApp sends are a real product need.
- The new service-message charge (from 1 October 2026) above 1,000 free per month mostly affects high-volume businesses, not typical SMBs.
- "More than 60% already send marketing messages via WhatsApp, often non-compliantly" points to a large base of app or personal-number users who could move to the compliant API through a simple Hebrew front end. This is an upsell and migration opportunity.

### Gaps
- No count of Israeli WhatsApp Business app users or WABA (API) accounts. Meta does not publish country figures.
- The official Meta Israel rate could not be confirmed; developers.facebook.com was not fetchable.
- A headline claiming "WhatsApp marketing messages are moving to a price you set" (maximum-price bidding) appeared but was not verified. — [AssistantLabs](https://assistantlabs.io/academy/guide/whatsapp-marketing-max-price)

## Q5. What do Israeli SMBs pay today for comparable tools and for agencies running Meta ads?

### Takeaway
Israeli SMBs pay roughly **₪40–300 per month** for self-serve marketing, messaging and CRM SaaS (often per user, before VAT, with annual discounts). They pay **₪1,200–7,000 per month** to an agency or freelancer to run Facebook/Instagram campaigns, or 10–20% of media spend for larger budgets. Marta sits between these two anchors.

### Cited Findings
- **Fireberry (formerly Powerlink), Israeli CRM:**
  - Free up to 250 contacts.
  - Paid plans ₪120–250 per user per month (annual billing, before VAT).
  - Professional: ₪150 annual / ₪180 monthly per user.
  - Enterprise: ₪250 annual / ₪300 monthly per user.
  — [Arbiter.co.il](https://arbiter.co.il/providers/fireberry/)
  - Fireberry Marketing has a free tier, with paid plans from about ₪45 per user per month (annual). — [Productland](https://productland.co.il/product/fireberry/)
  - Capterra lists Standard $35, Professional $45 and Enterprise $75 per user per month (≈ ₪130/₪167/₪278). — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/264979/Fireberry/pricing/)
  - Priority acquired Fireberry at a valuation above ₪100M (~$31M). — [Calcalist Tech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/ad88ae13j)
- **ActiveTrail (email/SMS/WhatsApp):**
  - Official pricing page: Basic at 50 per month on annual billing. The currency symbol was dropped in the extract; likely ₪.
  - A separate SMS price list includes 500 messages for ₪15. The extracted per-message figure (₪0.111) doesn't match ₪15/500 = ₪0.03, so verify.
  — [ActiveTrail pricing](https://www.activetrail.co.il/pricing/)
  - Capterra lists Basic $8, Plus $11 and Premium $298 per month. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/135244/ActiveTrail/)
- **Smoove (email/SMS marketing):**
  - Israeli comparison blogs give the basic plan as "from ₪39" or "₪49 per month", with a free tier; the sources conflict. — [Taglit.ai comparison](https://taglit.ai/%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%95%D7%90%D7%94/); [b-digital](https://b-digital.co.il/blog/email-marketing-compare)
  - Capterra lists $15 per user per month (≈ ₪56). — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/160863/Smoove/)
- **Glassix (Israeli omnichannel/WhatsApp inbox):** Starter $49 per user per month (≈ ₪181), Growth $65 (≈ ₪241). Listed in USD; the Tekpon listing is old. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/glassix/pricing); [Tekpon](https://tekpon.com/software/glassix/reviews/)
- **InforU:** No public pricing found.
- **Wix (Israeli website builder, USD pricing, 2026):**
  - Light $17 (annual) / $24 (monthly).
  - Core $29 / $36.
  - Business $36 / about $43–46.
  - Business Elite raised from $159 to $172 per month in April 2026.
  — [Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/cms/wix/); [Costbench changelog](https://costbench.com/changelog/wix-price-increase-2026-04/)
- **Agency and freelancer fees for Facebook/Instagram campaign management (2025–2026, Israeli agency pricing pages and guides; the search summary did not always map each figure to a specific page):**
  - Expert management typically ₪1,500–6,000 per month, depending on scope.
  - Basic FB/IG campaign management ₪1,200–2,500 per month; advanced ₪2,000–3,500 (excluding media budget).
  - Fixed monthly retainer ₪2,000–7,000 is the most common model; 10–20% of media budget is common for larger budgets.
  - One agency's packages: about ₪2,500–4,000 per month + VAT for Facebook/Instagram, and ₪7,000 including Google.
  - Typical ongoing retainer ₪1,500–5,000 per month.
  — [BuildoAI](https://buildoai.com/blog/facebook-ads-management-cost); [PaidAds.co.il](https://paidads.co.il/campaign-management/); [MediaDome](https://mediadome.co.il/pricing/campaigns/); [AirDigital](https://airdigital.co.il/digital-marketing-cost-israel/); [TZD-SEO](https://tzd-seo.org.il/facebook-prices/)
- **SMB media budgets (illustrative, not survey data):** a 2026 local-marketing guide uses roughly ₪1,000–1,800 per month on Meta as a typical Israeli small-business split. — [Datalatte](https://datalatte.pro/blog/local-marketing-israel-small-business-2026)
- **SaaS go-to-market costs (not Israel-specific):** 5W cites SMB SaaS customer acquisition cost of $300–800 with a 6–7 month payback. — [5W Research](https://www.5wpr.com/research/ai-israeli-marketing/)

### Inferences
- **Price anchors in ₪ per month, before VAT:**
  - Email/SMS tools: ₪40–60.
  - Website builder: ₪65–170.
  - CRM per user: ₪120–300.
  - Omnichannel inbox per user: ₪180–240.
  - Agency retainer: ₪1,500–6,000.
- **Recommended ARPU range for Marta (estimate):**
  - Solo / "boost-post" tier: ₪99–149 per month.
  - Core SMB tier (ads plus WhatsApp agent): ₪249–399 per month.
  - Agency tier (multiple client ad accounts): ₪790–1,490 per month.
  - WhatsApp message fees passed through at cost or with a small markup.
- If Marta replaces even part of a ₪2,000–4,000 per month freelancer retainer, ₪300–500 per month is easy to justify on value. The risk is that the cheapest local email/SMS tools (₪40–60) set SMB expectations of "a few dozen shekels".
- Fireberry charges about 20% more for monthly than annual billing (₪180 vs ₪150). Israeli SaaS commonly lists prices before VAT, with an annual-prepay discount; Marta could follow the same convention.

### Gaps
- No ILS price lists retrieved for Glassix, InforU, or Wix's Israeli checkout.
- No survey of what Israeli SMBs actually spend on SaaS per month. No SMB SaaS spending study from the Ministry of Economy, Israel Innovation Authority, Bank Hapoalim or Bank Leumi was found.
- No hard data on what share of Israeli SMB Meta advertisers outsource to agencies or freelancers versus doing it themselves.

## Q6. What characteristics of Israeli SMBs matter for selling SaaS (pricing, language, support, churn, billing)?

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin and mostly indirect. Israeli SMB software is priced in ILS before VAT, local vendors offer Hebrew support and free tiers, annual plans carry a discount, and churn is structurally high because many businesses are small and short-lived. No Israeli survey directly measures Hebrew-support preference, phone/WhatsApp support expectations or SaaS churn.

- Support via WhatsApp in Hebrew is likely expected, given 99% WhatsApp penetration and how local vendors operate. This is an inference with no direct survey evidence.

## Q7. Bottom-up sizing: how many target customers, at what ARPU, and what about expansion beyond Israel?

### Takeaway
By estimate, Israel has roughly **20,000–35,000 "always-on" SMB Meta advertisers**, plus **50,000–90,000 occasional advertisers and post-boosters**, plus about **2,000–5,000 small agencies and freelance campaign managers**. At plausible ARPUs, the Israeli serviceable market is about **₪170–315M a year (~$46–85M)**. A realistic 3-year obtainable share is **₪4–15M ARR (~$1–4M)**. Israel alone supports a solid local business, not a venture-scale one; expansion would need English/Arabic or other markets.

### Cited Findings
- **Business universe (2024):** ~685,000 businesses. 56.6% have no employees and 43.4% have employees; this split drives the employer-SMB count. — [OECD 2026](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/financing-smes-and-entrepreneurs-2026_075d8058-en/full-report/israel_e39ce641.html)
- **Meta's share of digital (2023–2025):**
  - Meta 25% of Israeli digital in 2023. — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/business/tech/article-1126831)
  - Meta ≈ 15% of a $1.58B digital market in 2025. — [5W Research](https://www.5wpr.com/research/ai-israeli-marketing/)
- **Typical SMB Meta budget (illustrative):** ₪1,000–1,800 per month. — [Datalatte](https://datalatte.pro/blog/local-marketing-israel-small-business-2026)
- **Agency count proxies:**
  - Dun's Guide lists 149 "advertising offices – general" and about 317 "advertising agencies". These are directory listings, not a census. — [Dun's Guide](https://www.dunsguide.co.il/L99995413_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9D/)
  - An undated ranking says the top 5 agencies manage about $341M of a roughly $784M ad pie, with 15 other large or mid-size agencies. — [Bviral](https://www.bviral.co.il/israel_marketing_company/)
- **Diaspora:**
  - About 1M Israelis live abroad (Knesset / Ministry of Aliyah estimate), up from 571,000–613,000 in 2021. — [Yeni Şafak citing official data](https://yenisafak.com/en/news/around-1-million-israelis-live-abroad-official-data-show-3705022)
  - JPR (March 2025) counts 955,000 "Israel-connected" people worldwide, including about 325,000 children born abroad. — [JPR](https://archive.jpr.org.uk/object-4449)
  - About 400,000 Jewish Israeli-Americans (ACS-based estimate). — [Research Square](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10894359/latest)
  - Emigration remained at record levels in 2025: TAU estimates 45,000–50,000 left for 12+ months. — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001551155); [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/r1zatkmnzg)

### Inferences

**Step 1: Target counts (ESTIMATES)**

| Segment | Basis | Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Employer SMEs (1–99 employees) | 685k × 42.9% (OECD 2024) | ~294,000 (hard-data derived) |
| Solo businesses (no employees) | 685k × 56.6% | ~388,000 (hard-data derived) |
| SMBs with any Meta ad spend in a year | 15–25% of employer SMEs (44–73k) + 5–10% of solo (19–39k) | **~63,000–112,000** (assumption) |
| "Always-on" SMB Meta advertisers (≥ ₪1,000/mo) | SMB Meta spend ₪0.2–0.45B/yr ÷ ₪12–18k/yr per advertiser | **~11,000–37,000; use 20,000–35,000** |
| Occasional advertisers / boosters | Remainder of the row above | **~50,000–90,000** |
| Small agencies + freelance campaign managers | Dun's ~466 formal listings as a floor; freelancers likely several times that | **~2,000–5,000** (assumption) |

The penetration method (63–112k) and the spend method (11–37k always-on) differ. That suggests most Israeli SMB "Meta advertisers" are low-spend or intermittent, and only about 20–35k are serious, always-on advertisers. The latter are Marta's core ICP, and they are the ones currently paying agencies.

**Step 2: ARPU assumptions (₪ per month before VAT; USD→ILS at 3.7)**
- Always-on SMB: ₪300 per month (₪3,600 a year). This sits between CRM per-user pricing (₪120–300) and the cheapest freelancer retainer (₪1,200–1,500).
- Occasional/booster SMB: ₪120 per month (₪1,440 a year). This is near local email/SMS tool pricing (₪40–60) plus a premium for AI and ads.
- Agency/freelancer: ₪1,000 per month (₪12,000 a year) for multi-client use.
- WhatsApp message fees are excluded (pass-through; about ₪0.13 per marketing message).

**Step 3: TAM / SAM / SOM, Israel (annual recurring revenue, ₪; USD at 3.7)**

| Layer | Definition | Count | ARPU (₪/yr) | Size (₪/yr) | Size (USD/yr) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | All employer SMEs (~294k) + ~30% of solo businesses that market to consumers (~116k) + agencies (~5k), all of whom could use a Meta/WhatsApp marketing assistant | ~415,000 | ₪2,400 blended for SMBs; ₪12,000 for agencies | **≈ ₪1.04B** (₪984M SMB + ₪60M agencies) | ≈ $280M |
| **SAM** | Businesses actively spending on Meta that a Hebrew-first SaaS can serve: always-on (20–35k) + occasional (50–90k) + agencies (2–5k) | ~72,000–130,000 | ₪3,600 / ₪1,440 / ₪12,000 | **≈ ₪170–315M** (midpoint ≈ ₪240M) | ≈ $46–85M |
| **SOM (yr 3)** | 3–5% of always-on + 1–2% of occasional + 5–10% of agencies | ~1,200–4,000 paying accounts | as above | **≈ ₪4–15M ARR** (midpoint ≈ ₪8.6M) | ≈ $1.1–4.0M |

**Explicit assumptions behind the table:**
1. Business counts come from OECD 2024 (685k; size shares as published). The 30% of solo businesses counted in TAM is an assumption.
2. Meta ad spend in Israel is ₪0.6–0.9B a year (derived from Bakara/Maariv 2023 and 5W 2025). SMBs are 35–50% of it (assumption; no Israeli data).
3. An always-on SMB spends ₪1,000–1,500 per month on Meta (illustrative guide, not survey data).
4. Meta ad penetration is 15–25% of employer SMEs and 5–10% of solo businesses (assumption; no Israeli advertiser count exists).
5. Agencies and freelancers number 2,000–5,000 (assumption; directory listings show ~466 formal agencies).
6. ARPUs are set against local anchors: CRM ₪120–300 per user, email/SMS ₪40–60, freelancer retainer ₪1,200–6,000.
7. SOM capture rates (3–5% / 1–2% / 5–10%) are typical early-stage SMB SaaS penetration assumptions over 3 years, not market data.
8. Before VAT. WhatsApp per-message fees excluded. Gross logo churn of about 3–6% a month in the SMB tier would need offsetting new sales to hold SOM.

**Labor pool Marta competes with (ESTIMATE):** if 50–70% of 20–35k always-on advertisers pay a freelancer or agency ₪2,000–4,000 per month, Israeli SMBs spend about **₪0.24–1.2B a year** on Meta campaign management labor. This pool is larger than the SaaS SAM, and Marta could capture part of it by automating that work.

**Expansion options:**
- **Hebrew-speaking diaspora:** about 1M Israelis abroad, including about 400k Jewish Israeli-Americans. The number of Israeli-owned SMBs abroad is unknown. This adds perhaps a modest fraction of the Israeli SAM and is not a growth engine.
- **Arabic-speaking Israeli SMBs and the Arab world/MENA:** Arabic support is the more scalable expansion route. Not sized here.
- **English:** global markets are dominated by Meta's own AI tools and many competitors.

### Gaps
- No hard count of active Meta advertisers in Israel. This is the single biggest uncertainty in the model. A Meta Ad Library scrape of Israeli advertisers, or a short survey through Israeli marketing Facebook groups, would tighten it.
- No count of Israeli freelance campaign managers or small digital agencies (a CBS industry-code count for advertising, NACE 73.1, would help).
- No count of Israeli-owned businesses abroad for the diaspora option.
- No data on Arab-Israeli SMB Meta advertising (relevant for a Hebrew-first product's coverage).
